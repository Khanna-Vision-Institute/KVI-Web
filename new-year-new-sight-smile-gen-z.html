Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SMILE take?

The laser portion is quick. Most visits are about an hour from check-in to check-out, including prep and post-op instructions.

When will I see results?

Many patients notice improvement in the first few days. Vision typically continues to refine over the following weeks as the eyes heal.

Is financing available?

Yes. Financing options may be available for qualified patients. We can also discuss using FSA/HSA when your plan allows for eligible vision correction.

What if I’m not a SMILE candidate?

No problem. Dr. Khanna offers multiple options (LASIK, PRK, ICL, PIE, and more). Your evaluation determines the safest, best-fit procedure for your eyes.
