your patients feel and see their best

Khanna Vision Institute works closely with optometrists, primary care physicians, and subspecialists across Greater Los Angeles. Our referral coordinators can assist with prioritized scheduling for complex cataract & keratoconus pathways, concierge lens consultations, urgent triage escalation, coordination of ancillary testing, and post-operative co-management pathways when medically appropriate — always aligning with how you wish to steward the patient relationship.

To make the referral experience easier for your office, KVI offers a dedicated Referral Coordinator (for physicians only) to help streamline communication and provide prompt support when you need it most. Your inquiry will be prioritized so our team can quickly assist with:

  • New patient onboarding & insurance-friendly documentation checklists
  • Procedure-specific consult templates (SMILE vs LASIK, EVO ICL candidacy)
  • Ongoing updates for referring offices when clinically requested
