Your First IRL Visit: The Real Deal

After your virtual consult, here's what going down when you actually come see us

Modern, clean, and nothing like those outdated clinics from the 2000s. We've got locations in Beverly Hills and Westlake Village—choose what's easiest for you.

Plan for about 90-120 minutes for your comprehensive eye exam. We're thorough but efficient. Bring your phone—our WiFi is solid.

We'll do a full workup: measurements, corneal mapping, refraction tests—basically we're getting every data point about your eyes. It's painless, just bright lights and some high-tech equipment.

You'll meet with Dr. Khanna again. He'll review your results, show you actual images of YOUR eyes (pretty wild), and customize a treatment plan specifically for you.

We break down all the costs—no hidden fees or surprise bills. We also have financing options because we know student loans are already a thing.

📱 What to Bring

ID, insurance card (if you have vision insurance), current glasses or contacts, and sunglasses for after (your eyes will be dilated).

Questions You're Probably Thinking

Is the virtual consult actually useful or just a sales pitch?
It's legit. Dr. Khanna will tell you if you're NOT a good candidate too. We'd rather be honest upfront than waste your time. You'll get real medical advice, not a hard sell.
Do I have to pay for the virtual consult?
Nope, it's completely free. No credit card needed to book. We just want to meet you and see if we can help.
How much does this all cost?
Depends on the procedure, but we'll break it all down during your consult. LASIK typically ranges from $2,000-$3,500 per eye. We offer financing options and accept HSA/FSA.
Am I too young for vision correction?
Generally you need to be 18+ with a stable prescription for at least a year. If you're younger or your prescription is still changing, we'll let you know when the time is right.
What if I'm scared of surgery?
Valid. Most people are nervous at first. The procedures are quick (like 10-15 minutes per eye), you're awake but numbed, and you'll see results immediately. We'll walk you through every single step so there are no surprises.
Can I bring someone to my first visit?
Absolutely! Bring your mom, your best friend, your partner—whoever makes you feel comfortable. Just note that since we dilate your eyes, you'll need someone to drive you home anyway.
