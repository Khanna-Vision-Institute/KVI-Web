Frequently Asked Questions

How long does PRK / SuperLASIK take?

The laser portion is quick—often minutes per eye. Most patients are in and out within about an hour.

When will I see results?

PRK improves gradually. Many patients notice functional improvement in the first week, with clarity continuing to sharpen over the following weeks. Full stabilization can take a few months.

Is financing available?

Yes—financing options are available for qualified patients. If you have FSA/HSA funds, ask our team how they may apply.

What if I'm not a candidate for PRK / SuperLASIK?

No problem. Dr. Khanna offers multiple options (SMILE, LASIK, EVO ICL, PIE, PRK). Your consultation determines the safest, best fit for your eyes. Results vary and candidacy is required.
