Why Home Compresses Fail (And Ours Works)

A chalazion is a blockage of a deep oil gland in your eyelid. The oil inside has hardened, like cold butter. Your warm washcloth at home simply can't get hot enough for long enough to melt this stubborn blockage.

The "Old Way" (Scary & Outdated)

The traditional method is "Incision & Drainage." This is a surgical procedure that involves:

  • ✕ A painful anesthetic injection in the eyelid.
  • ✕ Flipping the eyelid inside-out.
  • ✕ Using a scalpel to cut the eyelid open.
  • ✕ Scraping out the contents, causing trauma.
  • ✕ Risk of scarring and a long recovery.

Our "Modern Way" (Gentle & Effective)

Our advanced **Thermal Expression Procedure** is non-surgical and designed for your comfort:

  • ✔ Simple, painless numbing eye drops.
  • ✔ Applying therapeutic heat to melt the blockage.
  • ✔ Gently extracting the liquefied oil.
  • ✔ No incisions. No stitches. No scars.
  • ✔ Immediate relief and you drive home.

Your 3-Step Path to a Clear Eyelid

Our entire process is designed to be quick, comfortable, and highly effective.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the procedure hurt?

No. We use powerful anesthetic (numbing) eye drops to ensure you are completely comfortable. Most patients report feeling only a sense of warmth and light pressure, but no pain.

How long does the treatment take?

The entire in-office procedure is very quick, typically taking only 15-20 minutes from start to finish.

Is there any downtime?

No. You can drive yourself home and return to work or your normal activities the same day. Your eyelid may be slightly red or swollen for a day or two, but this is minor.

Will my chalazion come back?

While this procedure effectively clears the *current* blockage, chalazia are often a sign of an underlying condition called Meibomian Gland Dysfunction (MGD) or Blepharitis. We will also assess your overall eyelid health to create a plan to prevent new ones from forming.

Is this covered by insurance?

In many cases, the evaluation and treatment of a chalazion is considered medically necessary and is covered by PPO insurance plans. Our team will verify your benefits for you during your consultation.
