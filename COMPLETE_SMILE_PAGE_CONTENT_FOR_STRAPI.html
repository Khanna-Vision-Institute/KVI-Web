The Science Behind SMILE (Without the Medical Jargon)

Imagine reshaping your cornea through a keyhole instead of opening a door. That's SMILE. While LASIK creates a 20mm circular flap (basically a manhole cover on your eye), SMILE uses a tiny 2-4mm incision – smaller than a grain of rice.

Here's How It Actually Works:

Step 1: The Lenticule Creation (10 seconds)
The Zeiss VisuMax laser creates a thin, contact lens-shaped piece of tissue (lenticule) inside your cornea. No cutting, no pain, just precise laser pulses.
Step 2: The Extraction (13 seconds)
Dr. Khanna removes this lenticule through the micro-incision. Think of it like removing a contact lens from inside your cornea.
Step 3: Instant Reshape (0 seconds)
Your cornea immediately reshapes, correcting your vision. No flap to heal, no waiting, just results.

Total time? 23 seconds of laser. Less time than watching a TikTok.

Everything You're Wondering About SMILE

How is SMILE different from LASIK?

SMILE is fundamentally different – it uses only one laser to create and remove a lenticule through a 2-4mm incision, while LASIK uses two lasers and creates a 20mm flap. This means SMILE preserves corneal strength, causes 80% less dry eye, has no flap complications, and allows immediate return to activities. Think of it as keyhole surgery vs. open surgery for your eyes.

Is SMILE really worth the extra cost?

For most patients, absolutely. The extra $300-500 per eye buys you: 80% less dry eye issues, no flap complications ever, immediate return to sports/activities, stronger corneal integrity, and faster visual recovery. If you're active, have dry eyes, or work in physical jobs, SMILE pays for itself in peace of mind alone.

Can I really exercise the next day?

Yes! Light exercise like walking is fine immediately. Gym workouts, yoga, and running can resume at day 3. Swimming is cleared at 1 week. Contact sports are safe immediately since there's no flap to worry about. This is one of SMILE's biggest advantages over LASIK.

What if I blink or move my eye during surgery?

Impossible to mess up! Your eye is gently held open (you won't even feel it), and the laser tracks your eye movements 1,000 times per second. If you move beyond range, it automatically pauses and resumes when you're centered. The entire laser time is only 23 seconds, so there's barely time to worry!

How long do SMILE results last?

SMILE results are permanent. Your cornea doesn't "un-reshape" itself. The only vision changes you might experience are natural aging (presbyopia after 45) which affects everyone regardless of surgery. Studies show SMILE patients maintain stable vision for decades.

Can I get SMILE if I have astigmatism?

Yes! SMILE corrects astigmatism up to -3.00 diopters along with nearsightedness. In fact, SMILE's precision makes it excellent for astigmatism correction. About 40% of our SMILE patients have astigmatism corrected during their procedure.

What's the youngest age for SMILE?

FDA approval is for age 22 and above (slightly older than LASIK's 18+). This ensures your prescription is completely stable. If you're under 22 but eager for vision correction, we can discuss LASIK or waiting for SMILE eligibility.

Will I need reading glasses eventually?

After age 45, most people develop presbyopia (difficulty focusing up close) whether they've had surgery or not. This is natural aging of the lens, not related to SMILE. However, you'll only need readers for close work, not distance vision. We also offer solutions like monovision SMILE for those concerned about reading vision.
