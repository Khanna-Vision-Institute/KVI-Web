Pterygium Surgery FAQ

Indications, technique, recurrence prevention, recovery, risks, costs.

Related: Cosmetic Pterygium Back to FAQ hub
When should I consider surgery?
Symptomatic, progressive, vision-threatening, or cosmetic reasons after evaluation.
How do you reduce recurrence?
Autograft technique, UV protection, dryness control, and drop adherence.
Recovery timeline?
Days to desk work. Redness fades over weeks. Activity limits are written.
Glue vs sutures?
Chosen per case for stability and comfort.
Risks?
Recurrence, scarring, infection, graft issues, dryness flare.
