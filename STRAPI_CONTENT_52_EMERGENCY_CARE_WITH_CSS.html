Urgent Symptoms

  • Severe or increasing eye pain
  • Sudden drop in vision or new blur
  • Flashes, many floaters, curtain-like shadow
  • Chemical exposure or significant trauma
  • Persistent redness with light sensitivity
Usually Normal

Common Post-Op Sensations

  • Mild dryness or gritty feeling
  • Light sensitivity for a short time
  • Halo or glare at night early on
  • Fluctuating vision during healing

If unsure, contact us. We will advise next steps.

Emergency Care FAQs

Is the hotline truly 24/7?
Yes. After-hours calls are routed to on-call clinical support. If we miss you, we return calls promptly.
Can non-patients use this service?
The 24/7 line is for our active or recent post-op patients. New patients can book urgent visits during clinic hours.
Do I go to the ER or call you first?
For life-threatening issues or chemical burns, call 911 or go to the nearest ER first. Then notify us.
Do you offer video calls?
Yes. Telehealth helps triage fast. Some conditions still require in-person exams to protect vision.
