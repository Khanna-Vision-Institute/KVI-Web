Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SMILE take?

The laser portion is typically minutes per eye. Plan about an hour in the clinic for check-in, prep, and post-op instructions.

When will I see results?

Many patients notice vision improvement quickly, with clarity continuing to refine as the eyes heal. Your surgeon will set expectations based on your prescription and tear film.

Is financing available?

Yes—financing options may be available for qualified patients. Our team can also help you understand how FSA/HSA benefits may apply to vision correction.

What if I'm not a candidate for SMILE?

No problem. Dr. Khanna offers multiple advanced procedures (SMILE, LASIK, PRK, ICL, PIE). Your consultation focuses on the safest, best-performing option for your eyes.
