Cosmetic Pterygium Removal

Pre-operative checklist to get you ready for surgery

Pre-Op Instructions

These steps help ensure a smooth, safe cosmetic pterygium procedure and the best possible outcome.

Contact Lenses

  • Soft contact lenses: Stop wearing them 3-7 days before surgery.
  • Gas permeable / hard lenses: Stop wearing them 3-4 weeks before surgery (your doctor will confirm your exact timing).

No Scents or Fragrances

⚠️ Important: On the day of surgery, do not use perfume, cologne, aftershave, scented lotions, or hairspray. Fragrances can interfere with the laser and affect results.

Makeup & Skin Care

  • Remove all eye and facial makeup at least 24 hours before surgery.
  • Best practice: avoid eye makeup for 3 days before surgery.
  • Make sure there is no mascara or eyeliner residue along the lashes or lids.

Food, Medications, and Habits

  • Have a light meal on the day of your procedure.
  • Take your regular medications as instructed by your doctor.
  • No smoking on the day of surgery.
  • No alcohol on the day of surgery.

Transportation

  • Arrange for a responsible adult to drive you to and from the surgery center.
  • You may also need a driver for your 1-day post-op visit.

Artificial Tears (Preservative-Free)

Please purchase a 1-month supply of preservative-free artificial tears before surgery, such as:

  • Refresh Plus
  • Systane
  • Blink
  • Optive
💧

Preservative-Free Tears

Use as needed for comfort after surgery, especially during the first few weeks.

Antibiotic Ointment (Maxitrol or Equivalent)

Unless your doctor changes the plan, follow these general guidelines:

  • Apply to the operated eye(s) 3 times per day.
  • Start 1 day before surgery.
  • Continue for 5 days after surgery (or as directed).
🧴

Antibiotic Ointment

3×/day — start 1 day before surgery, continue for 5 days after.

What to Bring on Surgery Day

  • All prescribed eye drops and ointments.
  • A pair of dark sunglasses for the ride home.
  • Your arranged driver.
