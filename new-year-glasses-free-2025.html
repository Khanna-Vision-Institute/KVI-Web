Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SMILE take?

The laser time is typically about 10-15 minutes per eye. Plan about an hour total in the office for prep and check-in.

When will I see results?

Many patients notice clearer vision the same day or within 24 hours. Vision continues to refine over the next several weeks.

Is financing available?

Yes. We offer financing options (including 0% APR for qualified patients) and accept FSA/HSA. Plans can start around $89/month.

What if I'm not a candidate for SMILE?

That's common. Dr. Khanna offers multiple options (SMILE, LASIK, EVO ICL, PIE, PRK). Your consultation determines the safest best fit.
