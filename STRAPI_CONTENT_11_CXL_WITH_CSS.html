Can I wait a few months to decide? +

Honestly? Bad idea. Keratoconus progresses fastest in your 20s. Every month = more corneal damage that CAN'T be reversed. CXL stops progression but doesn't undo existing damage. Earlier treatment = better vision saved. We can get you in this week.

Will my vision be perfect after CXL? +

CXL's job is to STOP progression - mission accomplished in 96% of cases. Bonus: 30% of patients actually see improvement! You'll still need glasses/contacts but your prescription stops changing. Some patients later get ICL or other procedures once stable.

What's the difference between epi-on and epi-off? +

Epi-off (epithelium removed) is gold standard - deeper penetration, stronger cross-linking, FDA-approved. Recovery is 5-7 days. Epi-on (epithelium stays) has easier recovery but less effective. Dr. Khanna recommends epi-off for maximum protection. Short recovery worth lifetime of stable vision.

Can I do both eyes at once? +

Dr. Khanna typically does one eye at a time (2-4 weeks apart) so you can function during recovery. Some patients with mild KC do both together if they have good support at home. We'll discuss what's best for your lifestyle and KC severity.

How do I know if it worked? +

Corneal mapping at 3, 6, and 12 months shows if progression stopped (it will). Your prescription stabilizing is the biggest sign. No more frequent glasses changes! Most patients say the peace of mind alone is worth it - knowing your vision is protected.
