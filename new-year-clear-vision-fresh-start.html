Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SMILE take?

The laser portion is typically about 20–35 seconds. The full procedure is usually around 10–15 minutes per eye, and most visits are under an hour.

When will I see results?

Many patients notice clearer vision within 24 hours. Vision continues to refine over the following weeks as the eyes heal.

Is financing available?

Yes. We offer flexible financing options, including 0% APR plans for qualified applicants, and you can often use HSA/FSA funds. Approval and terms are set by the lender.

What if I'm not a candidate for SMILE?

No problem. Dr. Khanna offers multiple options (SMILE, LASIK, EVO ICL, PIE, PRK/ASA). Your consultation will confirm the safest, best-fit plan for your eyes.
