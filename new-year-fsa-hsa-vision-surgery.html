Frequently Asked Questions

How long does LASIK take?

The procedure is typically about 10-15 minutes per eye. Most patients are in and out in under an hour.

When will I see results?

Many patients notice clearer vision right away or within 24 hours. Vision can continue to stabilize over 1-3 months.

Can I use FSA/HSA and still finance?

Yes. We accept FSA/HSA funds, and 0% promo financing may be available for qualified applicants. We’ll walk you through the simplest way to pay.

What if I’m not a candidate for LASIK?

No problem. Dr. Khanna offers multiple options (SMILE, EVO ICL, PRK/ASA, PIE). Your consultation determines the safest, best match.
