The Real Talk: What Actually Happens During LASIK?

Forget the scary stories you've heard. Modern LASIK at our Beverly Hills and Westlake Village locations is literally a 10-minute Netflix episode per eye. Here's the actual play-by-play:

The Vibe Check (Pre-Surgery)

Before you even think about lying down on our state-of-the-art laser bed, we map your eyes with technology that makes your iPhone camera look basic. Our iDesign system creates a 3D blueprint of your eye that's 25 times more precise than traditional measurements. Think of it as Face ID for your cornea.

The Main Event

  1. Numbing drops – No needles, just drops that make your eye feel like it's had a spa day
  2. The flap creation – Using our femtosecond laser (fancy word for "incredibly precise"), we create a thin flap on your cornea in about 10 seconds
  3. The reshape – Our excimer laser reshapes your cornea based on your unique prescription (another 30-90 seconds)
  4. The finish – Flap goes back, starts healing immediately, no stitches needed

Total time? Less than it takes to order your Starbucks mobile order.

Breaking Down the Myths (Because TikTok Isn't Always Right)

Myth #1: "LASIK is painful AF"

Reality: You feel pressure for about 30 seconds. That's it. Most patients say their Brazilian wax hurt more.

Myth #2: "You can go blind from LASIK"

Reality: The FDA reports zero cases of blindness from LASIK when performed by board-certified surgeons like Dr. Khanna. You're statistically more likely to have complications from wearing contacts for 30 years.

Myth #3: "LASIK doesn't last"

Reality: Your cornea doesn't "un-reshape" itself. Once corrected, it stays corrected. Some people might need reading glasses after 45 (that's just aging, not LASIK wearing off).

Myth #4: "Recovery takes forever"

Reality: Most of our patients are back on their phones within hours and return to work the next day. Full recovery? 3-6 months, but you'll see clearly from day one.

Everything You Want to Know About LASIK

Can I watch screens immediately after LASIK?

Yes! Most patients can use phones and computers within hours. We recommend taking breaks every 20 minutes for the first few days and using preservative-free eye drops.

Will LASIK affect my night driving?

Some patients experience temporary halos around lights for 1-3 months. With our iDesign technology, less than 1% have permanent night vision issues. Most say their night vision improves!

Can I wear makeup after LASIK?

Eye makeup should wait 1 week, face makeup is OK after 3 days. Time to show off those eyes without glasses covering them!

What if I blink or move during surgery?

Impossible to mess up! We use a gentle eyelid holder, and our laser tracks your eye 4,000 times per second. If you move, the laser automatically pauses.

Can I get LASIK if I have astigmatism?

Absolutely! LASIK corrects nearsightedness, farsightedness, AND astigmatism. Dr. Khanna specializes in complex prescriptions.

How soon can I exercise after LASIK?

Light exercise like walking is fine immediately. Gym workouts can resume after 1 week. Swimming and contact sports after 1 month. We'll give you a detailed timeline!
