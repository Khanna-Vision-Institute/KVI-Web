Frequently Asked Questions

How long does PIE take?

The procedure itself is typically about 15 minutes per eye. Plan on roughly 2 hours in the office for prep and recovery.

When will I see results?

Most patients notice clearer vision quickly and can often drive within a few days. Your vision continues to refine over 1–3 months.

Is financing available?

Yes. We offer financing options for qualified patients, and many patients use FSA/HSA funds when eligible.

What if I’m not a candidate for PIE?

Your consultation is surgeon-led and personalized. If PIE isn’t the best fit, Dr. Khanna will guide you to the right option for your eyes and goals.
