Frequently Asked Questions

How is EVO ICL different from the older ICL versions?

EVO ICL features a central port that allows natural fluid flow, eliminating the need for peripheral iridotomy in most cases. It also has improved optics and a larger optical zone for better night vision.

Will I feel the lens in my eye?

No, the EVO ICL is completely undetectable once implanted. It sits behind your iris where you cannot feel it, similar to how you don't feel your natural lens.

Can EVO ICL correct astigmatism?

Yes! The Toric EVO ICL can correct both myopia and astigmatism up to 6 diopters, providing comprehensive vision correction.

How long does the EVO ICL last?

The EVO ICL is designed to be permanent but can be removed or replaced at any time. The lens material doesn't degrade, so it can last a lifetime.

What about future cataract surgery?

The EVO ICL doesn't prevent or complicate future cataract surgery. If needed, the lens can be easily removed during cataract surgery.
